The easy half

Making it look like cinema

AI skill gets you attractive images. That part is packed in too — camera work written so it reads photographic rather than generated, and so it adapts to the genre of the story being told. Thirteen lenses, eight stops, a full shot ladder, chosen per shot instead of globally.

The hard half

Knowing what to shoot

Shot breakdown, staging, rhythm. Three quarters of the directors working in advertising cannot do this — and people who only know generation certainly cannot. It is the part that decides whether a film cuts together. It is the part that is now in the tool.

It doesn't raise the quality of what you make. It raises the quality of what everyone in the process makes.
